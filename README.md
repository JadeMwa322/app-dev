# Gagamboy

**"Gagamboy"** is a 2004 Filipino superhero comedy film directed by Erik Matti. It stars Vhong Navarro as Junie, an ice cream vendor who accidentally swallows a spider exposed to chemicals, gaining web-slinging powers and becoming the titular hero, Gagamboy. The film humorously parodies superhero tropes, including a rivalry with Dodoy (played by Jay Manalo), who transforms into "Ipisman" (Cockroachman) after eating a contaminated sandwich
